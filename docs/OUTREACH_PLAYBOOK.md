# Outreach Playbook

Core message:

> You already have local demand showing up through Google, reviews, and search. The problem is that your website, booking flow, reviews, and follow-up system are not capturing enough of it. We build the system that turns local attention into calls, appointments, quote requests, and repeat customers.

Use local, direct, short copy. Mention one or two specific audit observations. Avoid insulting the business. Keep every draft for manual use only.

Strong first angles:

- No website linked from Google: owned website plus quote/contact capture.
- Social-only web presence: full website plus tracking and conversion path.
- Many reviews but weak website: trust-to-conversion package.
- Appointment business with no booking: conversion website plus scheduling and reminders.
- High-ticket trade with slow/manual intake: speed-to-lead trade growth system.
