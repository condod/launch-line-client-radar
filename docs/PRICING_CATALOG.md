## Why These Prices Are Competitive

The client is buying implementation, configuration, testing, documentation, and handoff instead of a large mandatory agency retainer. Current entry-level platform pricing supports a lower-overhead model:

- Zapier offers a free tier and lists Professional from $19.99/month: https://zapier.com/pricing
- Calendly lists a free plan and Standard at $10 per seat/month: https://calendly.com/pricing
- HubSpot offers free tools and currently advertises promotional Starter pricing from $7 per seat/month annually or $10 monthly: https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm/starter
- MailerLite lists a free tier, Comfort from $12/month, and Power from $25/month: https://www.mailerlite.com/pricing
- Tidio lists entry customer-service automation from $24.17/month: https://www.tidio.com/pricing
- Twilio US SMS starts at $0.0083 per segment before phone-number, carrier, and registration fees: https://www.twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing/us

Vendor prices and plan limits can change. Confirm the exact platform and usage cost before quoting a client.

## Operating Rules

- The client owns and pays for every production software account.
- The setup fee covers the written scope and one tested launch.
- Optional care covers health checks and minor fixes, not unlimited revisions or new workflows.
- Every workflow needs failure alerts, test records, and a named human owner.
- SMS and email automations require client-approved content, consent handling, opt-out behavior, and applicable legal review.
- Review requests must be neutral and sent consistently, without gating, incentives, or pressure.
- No automation is described as maintenance-free. The listed review cadence is part of the handoff.
