# Sales Offer

## Positioning

Launch Line Client Radar helps local service providers find businesses with visible demand but weak owned lead capture. The product is not a generic lead list. It connects public business signals, owned website evidence, deterministic scoring, manual outreach copy, and proposal creation in one local-first workspace.

## Sellable Outcomes

- Prioritized local prospect database.
- Website-first audit evidence for each lead.
- Recommended Launch Line package and first service to pitch.
- Pipeline board for manual sales follow-up.
- Client-ready proposal draft.
- JSON backup and CSV export for handoff.
- iPad-friendly standalone demo.

## Suggested Packages To Sell

### Research Sprint

Deliver 50-100 scored local prospects, top 20 manual audit notes, and CSV handoff.

Suggested price: $750-$1,500.

### Website-First Growth Audit

Deliver a scored prospect list plus proposal-ready recommendations for selected businesses.

Suggested price: $1,500-$3,500.

### Managed Launch Line Pipeline

Run weekly collection, audit, prioritization, manual outreach preparation, and proposal support.

Suggested price: $500-$1,500/month plus implementation retainers.

## Demo Script

1. Open Dashboard and show hot leads, website missing count, and territory map.
2. Open Lead Database and filter by missing website or minimum score.
3. Select one lead and show the evidence, score breakdown, and package recommendation.
4. Open Audit Workspace and toggle evidence to show live rescoring.
5. Open Pipeline Board and move the lead from Researched to Contacted.
6. Open Offer Builder and copy or export the proposal.
7. Open Export Center and create a JSON backup.

## Non-Negotiables

- All sample prospects must remain fictional unless collected by the operator.
- Claims must be based on verified public evidence.
- Outreach must be manually reviewed and sent by a human.
- Do not promise rankings, reviews, revenue, or lead volume.
